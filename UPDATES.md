# Updates

## September 2026

- Expanded the collection with [Show-Harness](https://github.com/showlab/Show-Harness), [Cortex / InternVLA-M1.5](https://github.com/InternRobotics/Cortex), [GPT-Policy](https://github.com/cheng-haha/GPT-Policy), and [GPT-6 Astra as an Embodied Policy](https://github.com/anonymous-report-421/GPT-as-Policy), preserving the distinction between public project claims and independently verified robot results.
- Added [RoboDawn](https://robodawn.top/), [Agent as Policy](https://agent-as-policy-2026.github.io/), and [SafeHarness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20822), extending coverage from closed-loop VLM control to coding agents and explicit safety-aware execution.
- Added [FailBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03611), [ENACT](https://enact-embodied-cognition.github.io/), and [RoboDojo](https://github.com/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo) as evaluation references; retained RoboDojo's September benchmark corrections and result-stability note.
- Updated [FluxVLA](https://github.com/FluxVLA/FluxVLA) with its September GPT-6/GR00T N1.7 support and [Inspect Robots](https://github.com/robocurve/inspect-robots) with v0.58.0 agent-camera, scorer API, and run-record updates; added [StationeryBench](https://github.com/robocurve/stationerybench) as a five-task bimanual benchmark with clear mock-versus-real evidence boundaries.
- Strengthened the GPT-6 Astra constraint demonstration with official Dual ALOHA kinematic-replay evidence and a preview image, while retaining its non-hardware evidence boundary.
